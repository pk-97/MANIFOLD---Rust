Of the 49 TEST-ONLY rows (35 node-engine, 14 nodes-scene), 4 sit inside candidates (`remove_node` graph-018, `binding_value` graph-019, `prewarm_ids` graph-021, `default_sun` graph-025), 1 belongs to graph-026 (`is_relight_node_id`), and 44 are judged in the proof file's rejected section.

The census tool counts a type's own `impl` block as a use, so it files a wholly dead module under CRATE-ONLY. The dead modules graph-001 to graph-004 and graph-020 were found by following DEAD rows inside them (`resize_vertices`, `dispatch_a_only`, `read_texture`, `uniform_arena_mut`) and re-searching the owning type. The proof file shows how.
