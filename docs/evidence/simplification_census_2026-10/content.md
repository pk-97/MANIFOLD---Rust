## Pure design issues for the UX pass

No slop root found; these are layout and interaction choices.

- The card's Physics heading is a link to the Scene tab (10:26).
- Focus Distance dims on the card while depth of field is off: that is the designed `Gate::Dim` (`scene_surface.rs:297-300`). In the Camera node the depth-of-field settings sit above their switch because the switch lives on `node.bokeh_gather` and the settings on `node.camera_lens` (0:47, 6:16).
- Reset row has only an A button; Sun row has a white square and no unit (0:47).
- Slider range behind a right-click menu; the mapping panel lives in the graph editor (5:22).
- One scroll for tree and settings (1:03 to 3:45); tree group headings and items look alike, and a Physics group holds a Physics item (3:34).
- Hex entry sits in the palette menu on the R row only (3:09).
- "Glass" is a material look and an optional feature: looks are recipes over features (`manifold-app/src/ui_bridge/material_looks.rs:8-33`), not a duplicate param (1:23).
- Add Coat on water changes little: it seeds clearcoat 1.0 on an already glossy surface (2:35).
- Choices and switches drawn as sliders: the uniform slider row stays by Peter's ruling (1:23 to 7:08).
- The clip and generator stay named "Physics Boxes" after every box is removed (5:41, 9:56).
- Audio Setup defaults for a new source and a new audio layer (8:27, 8:47).
- Trigger lane context menu, collapsed layer hides its lanes, DRAW and RESTORE ALL, folding every drawer, graph editor scroll and node size, the empty Clip tab, missing tooltips.
